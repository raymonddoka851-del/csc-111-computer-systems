# CSC 111 · Specific question bank

Attempt each question before opening the [worked answer key](ANSWER_KEY.md). Show your reasoning, steps, and checks. IDs are used in the progress tracker.

## 01 · Foundations

**Q01.** A student types three quiz scores, calculates their mean, and saves it. Identify input, processing, output, and storage in this example.

**Q02.** Convert `10110₂` to decimal. Show the place values and addition.

**Q03.** Compare first-generation and fourth-generation computers by their principal technology and one practical consequence of the change.

## 02 · Hardware

**Q04.** A user is editing a report, has 8 GB of free disk space, but many programs are open and the computer is slow. Explain the difference between RAM and storage and identify a likely resource bottleneck.

**Q05.** For scanning a printed multiple-choice answer sheet, choose OCR or OMR and justify the choice. What would be suitable for converting a paragraph on the page into editable text?

**Q06.** Trace how a key press becomes a calculated result shown on screen. Name the major hardware units in order.

## 03 · Software

**Q07.** Classify each item as system software or application software: Windows, a printer driver, a backup utility, and Excel. Explain each classification.

**Q08.** Write pseudocode to convert a temperature in Celsius to Fahrenheit using `F = C × 9/5 + 32`. Test it with `C = 20`.

**Q09.** A message claims to be an invoice, urges immediate action, and includes an unexpected attachment. Identify two warning signs and give a safe response sequence.

## 04 · Windows and files

**Q10.** Give the steps to create `CSC111\Week1` in Documents, save `review.docx` there, and verify it was saved in the right place.

**Q11.** Explain the difference between copying, moving, and creating a shortcut to a file. Which method preserves the original and creates a separate file copy?

**Q12.** Your computer is unresponsive but the sign-in screen is available. Compare Lock, Sign out, and Restart; choose the appropriate action if you are stepping away briefly.

## 05 · Internet and email

**Q13.** Explain how the Internet, the Web, a browser, and a search engine relate to one another.

**Q14.** You need a reliable, current source for a health statistic. List four checks you would make before using and citing a search result.

**Q15.** You are emailing a course announcement to 30 students and do not want them to see one another’s addresses. Which recipient field should you use, and what checks should you complete before sending?

## 06 · Word processing

**Q16.** Give a procedure for creating a two-section report with a title, consistent headings, page numbers, and a saved `.docx` file.

**Q17.** Why are heading styles and page breaks preferable to manually bolding headings and pressing Enter repeatedly? Give two reasons.

**Q18.** Outline the steps and two safety checks for a mail merge that sends personalized letters from a spreadsheet list.

## 07 · Spreadsheets and Excel

**Q19.** Cells `B2:B5` contain 12, 18, 15, and 5. Give Excel formulas for the sum and average, and calculate both.

**Q20.** Cell `B1` contains a tax rate of 0.16 and `C2` contains a price of 250. Write a formula in `D2` to calculate tax using the rate in `B1`, then give the formula after it is copied to `D3`.

**Q21.** You sort a table by surname but only the surname column moves. Explain the resulting problem and the correct way to sort. Name one useful chart for comparing sales across four products.

## 08 · Presentations and networks

**Q22.** Plan a four-slide presentation on computer safety: give each slide a purpose and one design choice that improves clarity.

**Q23.** A campus office network connects computers, a shared printer, and an Internet gateway. Identify the network scale/type, explain the roles of a switch and router, and state one benefit.

**Q24.** Give two benefits and two risks of networks, then pair each risk with a practical safe-use measure.
